& Why we exist

Progress is accelerating. Protection and opportunity are not.

Artificial intelligence and emerging technology are reshaping how people work, learn, access services, and make decisions. But access, protection, and opportunity are not advancing equally.

Communities already facing economic and social barriers are often the least represented in how new technologies are designed, governed, and deployed.

Solve & Serve works to close that gap — through education, research, community partnerships, and responsible innovation.

& What we do

Three ways we put technology to work for people.

Every program we build starts with a real problem affecting a real community — and ends with a solution designed to last.

Human-Centered AI & Digital Protection

Protect people

We help communities understand and respond to the risks created by AI, automation, surveillance, misinformation, and digital exclusion — advancing human-centered approaches to safety, privacy, and rights.

Community Technology Access

We help underserved communities gain practical access to the tools, knowledge, and infrastructure needed to participate — and compete — in the changing digital economy.
